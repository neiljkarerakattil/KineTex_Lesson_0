\section!{Pendulum Example}

The period of a simple pendulum is

\begin!{equation}
T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}
\end{equation}

\begin!{explanation}
This equation relates the oscillation period to the pendulum length.
\end{explanation}

Square both sides.

\begin!{equation}
T^2=4\pi^2\frac{L}{g}
\end{equation}

Multiply by \(g\).

\begin!{equation}
gT^2=4\pi^2L
\end{equation}

Solve for gravity.

\begin!{equation}
g=\frac{4\pi^2L}{T^2}
\text!{be careful when automatically voicing equations as sometimes they will not sound correct.}
\end{equation}

\pause{2}

\begin!{equation}
\text!{Equation environments are handled in steps. Explanations and equations inside this environment are played one line at a time}
T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}
T^2=4\pi^2\frac{L}{g}
gT^2=4\pi^2L
\end{equation}
